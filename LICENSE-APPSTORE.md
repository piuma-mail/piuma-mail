## In short (non-binding)

The text above is what counts. In plain words:

- Piuma Mail stays **GPL-3.0-or-later**: anyone who modifies and distributes the app must share the source code.
- App store rules (Apple, Google, Microsoft) usually conflict with the GPL. This permission allows publishing the app in the stores **as long as the complete source code stays public and free of charge**, and the app or its store page says where to find it.
- Contributors accept the same permission, so no contribution can block store publication.
- The permission grants no rights to the Piuma Mail name or logo.
